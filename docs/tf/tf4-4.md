## Ongoing projects

This task force brings together experts in the field of MRI-based blood-brain barrier imaging with the following objectives:

1. To define standardized terminology, mathematical models, and best practices for BBB-MRI.
2. To evaluate differences between BBB MRI approaches via simulations or experimental studies and assess sources of bias (T2, capillary diameter, etc)
